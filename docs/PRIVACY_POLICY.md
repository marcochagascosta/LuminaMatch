# Privacy Policy — Lumina Match

**Last updated:** 2026-08-27  
**Developer:** MarcoSaas / Marco Costa  
**Contact:** marcochagascosta@gmail.com · canal de suporte nas listagens da App Store / Google Play.

## Summary

Lumina Match is a casual match-3 game. Progress (lives, coins, boosters, levels, settings) is stored **locally on your device**. This version does not require an account.

## Data we process

### On device
- Game progress and settings (local storage / PlayerPrefs)
- Optional purchase receipts processed by Apple / Google when you buy in-app items

### Advertising (when ads are shown)
- We use **Google AdMob** to show rewarded and interstitial ads.
- AdMob and Google may process advertising identifiers, coarse device/app info, and interaction data according to [Google’s privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and AdMob terms.
- If you purchased **Remove Ads**, rewarded/interstitial ads from AdMob are not shown.

### Not collected by us as a custom backend
- No custom analytics server operated by MarcoSaas
- No account registration / login
- No precise location tracking by the game itself
- No contact list access

### Third parties
- **Apple App Store / Google Play** process payments for in-app purchases
- **Google AdMob** serves ads (see above)

## Children

The game is intended for a general puzzle audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children beyond what the platform / AdMob may process under their policies. Parental guidance applies for in-app purchases.

## Your choices

- Delete the app to remove local progress
- Manage purchases in your Apple ID / Google account settings
- Limit ad tracking via iOS / Android system settings (ATT / ads privacy)
- Purchase Remove Ads to disable AdMob interstitial/rewarded ads in the game

## Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date will change when we do.
